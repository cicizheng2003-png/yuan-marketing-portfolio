YUAN ZHENG PORTFOLIO — NETLIFY READY

1. Go to Netlify and choose Add new project > Deploy manually.
2. Drag THIS ENTIRE FOLDER (yuan_portfolio_netlify_final) into the upload area.
   You can also drag the ZIP, but dragging the unzipped folder is the safest option.
3. Netlify will publish it and give you a public URL.

Important:
- Keep index.html at the root of this folder.
- Keep assets/, video/, and docs/ next to index.html.
- Hero image is the full-resolution 1672 × 941 source, not the low-resolution Vercel version.
- The 2-minute video has been web-optimized to 1280 × 720 (~12 MB) for faster loading while remaining clear.

Main files:
- index.html: page structure/copy
- styles.css: design/layout
- script.js: case interactions
- assets/hero-cover.png: full-resolution cover
- assets/background-full.png: full-resolution background
- video/vitruvian-pitch-edit.mp4: optimized playable video
